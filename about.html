About Arcadia

Arcadia is a climate-technology company that pairs energy and emissions data infrastructure with hands-on decarbonisation consulting — bringing together the data platform with the advisory expertise of ENGIE Impact, now part of Arcadia.

That combination helps the world's largest companies move from measurement to action: connecting utility, energy and emissions data at scale, then translating it into transition plans and targets that stand up to standards like the SBTi Corporate Net-Zero Standard. In short — the data to know where emissions are, and the consulting to actually reduce them.
