Three routes to net-zero scope 1

C10.2a — A straight-line absolute emissions cut from your base year down to residual levels by 2050. This is the only route where a long-term target stays optional: set the near-term target and you are compliant. Best suited to companies without an applicable sector pathway.

C10.2b — Align scope 1 to a published sector decarbonisation pathway (steel, cement, chemicals and similar), measured as emissions per unit of physical output. A long-term target is mandatory, and the transition plan is due at validation rather than after a grace period.

C10.2c — New in V2.0. Instead of an emissions curve, you commit to a dated schedule to abate, retire or replace specific emitting assets, documented in an Asset Decarbonization Plan. Long-term target mandatory; designed for asset-heavy operators.

Routes can be mixed; multiple targets aggregate into one communication-only consolidated figure.
The fine print that bites

The intensity and asset routes lose V1's 15-month grace period — the transition plan must publish at Target Validation. Where relevant, plans must commit to phasing out revenue from unabated fossil fuels. Bioenergy emissions are reported separately from removals with no netting, following the GHG Protocol Land Sector & Removals guidance (LSRS).
