SBTi Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2.0 · June 2026

Scope 3 is no longer a coverage rule.
It's a decision system.

V1 asked one question: have you covered two-thirds of your value chain emissions? V2.0 asks four, in sequence. Work through them stage by stage - and tap any category in stage 2 to see its eligible target levers from Table 3.

1

Build the boundary

The 5% test decides what's in. Everything left out needs receipts - condition cited, tonnes quantified, mitigation intent described.

In boundary
Every scope 3 category that individually represents 5% or more of scope 3 emissions (categories 1–14), measured on the physical GHG inventory.

3

Deliver through the hierarchy

Implementation is now ranked. Each step down the ladder needs a demonstrated reason - structural constraints, never preference or cost alone.

Activity level Cut at source: efficiency, input switching, direct supplier and customer engagement.
Activity pools Act inside shared systems - supply sheds, grids, logistics networks - supported by instruments for biomethane, steel or cement via mass balance or book-and-claim.
Sector level Only while constraints persist: same activity type, relevant geography, plus measures to unlock direct action.

In-inventory reductions earn company-level claims. Pool and sector actions earn system contribution claims - reported separately, never blended.
