Two ways to set the target

C12.2a — Grow the share of low-carbon electricity (renewables, nuclear, and CCS-fitted generation) on a linear path; the long-term version reaches 100% LCE. Category A companies also submit electricity demand projections at validation.

C12.2b — Cut scope 2 emissions linearly to a residual level; the long-term option lands at residual by 2050. Long-term targets stay optional on this route, but Category A still files electricity demand projections at validation.

Category A also submits electricity demand projections at validation; long-term targets stay optional.
Procurement rules of the road

C29–C31 — The implementation hierarchy ranks activity-level actions first: cut consumption, add behind-the-meter generation, then contract for low-carbon electricity inside your deliverability region. Eligible instruments include physical and virtual PPAs, supplier LCE contracts, unbundled EACs, and default-delivered LCE. Generators must be 15 years old or younger; PPAs get flexibility where offtake starts within 36 months of commissioning, and contracts signed before February 2027 are grandfathered.

Hourly matching - the new transparency layer

C32–C34 — Hourly matching is a new transparency layer. Recognition thresholds rise from 50% (until 2030) to 75% (until 2035) to 90% (from 2035). Reporting is mandatory for Category A companies in any activity pool over 10 GWh, with third-party assurance. Meeting the thresholds earns optional recognition on the SBTi Dashboard.
